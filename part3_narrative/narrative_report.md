## Chart Choice Justifications

1. **"Which month had the highest total revenue?"**
   - **Chart Type**: Vertical Bar Chart (Column Chart)
   - **Justification**: This is a univariate comparison across 3 discrete time periods (April, May, June). A column chart starting at a zero baseline clearly communicates which month peaked within seconds without requiring extra visual complexity.

2. **"What percentage share does Ethnic Wear represent of April's total revenue?"**
   - **Chart Type**: 100% Stacked Bar Chart or Donut Chart
   - **Justification**: This is a univariate part-to-whole relationship question. A single-stack bar chart or donut chart makes the ~24.92% composition immediately visible while strictly avoiding misleading 3D pie charts.

3. **"How do the four regions compare on total revenue?"**
   - **Chart Type**: Horizontal Bar Chart
   - **Justification**: This is a bivariate categorical comparison (Region vs Total Revenue) across 4 regions. A horizontal bar chart ranked in descending order allows direct, unambiguous comparison between North, West, South, and East.
